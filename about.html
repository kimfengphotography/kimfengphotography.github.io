Background

Kim Feng Cheong (b. 2001) is from Whadjuk and Bindjareb land / Perth, WA, and is based in Wurundjeri and Boonwurrung land / Melbourne, VIC. Cheong's family is Chinese-Serani from Malaysia and Brunei.
